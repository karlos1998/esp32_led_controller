Touge Light

Privacy Policy and Content Rights

Touge Light is a simple iOS app for controlling an ESP32-based LED controller over Bluetooth Low Energy. The app is designed to work locally on your device and does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Touge Light does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not send personal data to the developer's servers.

Local App Data

The app may store the following settings locally on your device so that your LED controller can remember your preferred state:

  • selected LED color,
  • LED on/off state,
  • brightness level,
  • recently selected colors.

This information stays on your device unless you remove the app or your operating system backs up app data according to your device settings.

Bluetooth and Device Permissions

Touge Light uses Bluetooth Low Energy to scan for and connect to a compatible ESP32 LED controller. The app may request Bluetooth permission so it can discover nearby BLE devices and send color or brightness commands to the LED controller.

Depending on the operating system version and app build, iOS may show additional permission prompts related to local device discovery. These permissions are used only to connect to and control the LED hardware. Touge Light does not use these permissions to determine your physical location.

Data Sent to the LED Controller

When connected, the app sends simple LED control values, such as color and brightness commands, over Bluetooth to the compatible LED controller. These commands are used only for controlling the lighting device.

Third-Party Services, Ads, and Tracking

Touge Light does not include advertising, analytics, social media tracking, or third-party tracking services. The app uses standard Flutter libraries and Bluetooth functionality required for the app to operate.

Children's Privacy

Touge Light is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support or contact information provided on the Touge Light App Store product page.

The Touge Light app interface, app name, text, layout, and related app materials are created for this app or used under appropriate licenses. The app does not contain user-generated content, media feeds, or third-party copyrighted content that users can browse, upload, download, or share.

Icons and Framework Assets

The app may use standard platform and framework-provided interface elements, such as Material icons and Flutter components, under their applicable licenses. These assets are used only as part of the app's normal user interface.

Hardware Control

Touge Light controls a compatible ESP32 LED device over Bluetooth. It does not stream, display, distribute, or provide access to protected third-party content. The Bluetooth commands generated by the app are limited to LED color, brightness, and on/off control.
